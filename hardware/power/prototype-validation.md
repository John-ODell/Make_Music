## Bench sequence and acceptance

Use an isolated, adjustable current-limited DC supply as the cell emulator, an electronic load, DMM and scope. All probe grounds reference H1.2/GND; never attach a grounded clip to H1.1. Use accessible named-net pads, not hand probing between U2's fine pins. Verify isolation before reversing the emulator with USB present. Measure current in series; changing meter range/connections is done with sources OFF.

Keep the real cell and external charger out of all tests below. Connect/disconnect Pico/modules with every source OFF. Before each ramp record **H1 voltage and BAT_SW_PLUS at U2 IN separately**. The latter controls UVLO/OVLO. Stop on incorrect polarity, supply current-limit operation during a normal test, unexpected heating, rail excursions or failure to latch; identify the cause before increasing a limit.

1. **Cold checks / first power:** no Pico, USB or modules. Verify continuity against the final netlist, no power-to-ground short, correct resistor values and no VBUS_USB/battery bridge. Switch OFF; set emulator 4.20 V, 20 mA limit. Switch ON, inspect initial no-load current/VSYS; engineering screening target <5 mA bare-board steady current, no CC. VSYS should follow valid IN with low drop. Switch OFF and confirm input-branch interruption. A continuity-meter beep on capacitors is not sufficient evidence of a sustained short.
2. **Battery-path load:** raise supply limit to 0.75 A only after cold/first-power pass. With no Pico/USB, use VSYS electronic load at 0.10, 0.35 and 0.70 A; record H1, IN, VSYS and component temperatures. At 0.70 A require IN-to-VSYS drop ≤65 mV (31.5 mV U2 model plus 30 mV copper allowance, rounded), no unintended latch, stable output and resistor local temperature ≤70°C. This is a prototype design criterion, not a new datasheet guarantee. Verify input current ≤0.70 A in the eventual normal instrument configuration; the bare-path 0.70 A output test adds input-side quiescent current and is characterization, not approved normal loading.
3. **UVLO:** replace load with 100 Ω, ≥1 W from VSYS to GND; supply limit 100 mA. Start at 4.20 V, lower slowly to 2.90 V, then raise to 4.25 V. Measure actual IN at disconnect and restart: expected calculation ranges **3.053–3.430 V falling /3.363–3.752 V rising**, nominal 3.221/3.546 V. A 3.10 V point may legitimately be OFF. UVLO recovers automatically; distinguish it from fault latch. Record loaded H1 cutoff as well, since harness/fuse losses shift the apparent cell endpoint.
4. **Fault latch, no USB/Pico/modules:** return to valid 4.25 V emulator input, confirm IN stays above the measured UVLO threshold. Set supply limit 1.30 A for this isolated test only. Pulse electronic load from 0.70 toward 1.20 A in increments, each above-0.70 A pulse ≤20 ms, recording current immediately before output collapse with a current probe or calibrated shunt/scope; a DMM alone cannot capture trip. Engineering qualification target **0.80–1.20 A**, with stable IN; stop if 1.25 A demand fails to trip. Return load to 0.10 A: output must stay off until reset. Repeat after reset at H1=3.70 V and a lower-voltage point with IN ≥0.10 V above the measured falling UVLO throughout the pulse; record each actual IN. If the supply enters CC or IN crosses UVLO, the result is invalid, not proof of breaker action. Published current bounds were specified at VIN=12 V; this test supplies the missing 1S evidence. Do not intentionally blow F1 or hard-short a live cell.
5. **Reset / OVLO:** remove load fault and all sources, S1 OFF. Confirm **BAT_SW_PLUS <1.0 V and EN_UVLO <0.45 V** before reapplying power; record the actual OFF dwell. Do not promise immediate switch cycling resets charged input bypass, especially with USB still on. With no Pico/USB/modules and the 100 Ω load, use a 100 mA-limited emulator to ramp IN toward **5.45 V maximum**, then downward. This deliberately exceeds 1S voltage only on the bare carrier. Expected OVLO trip **4.710–5.315 V**, recovery **4.275–4.860 V**. Record actual IN and waveform; no inference from a typical response time that arbitrary surges are safe.
6. **Reverse insertion / USB isolation:** no Pico initially. S1 OFF; configure the isolated emulator so H1.1 is negative relative to H1.2, magnitude 4.25 V, limit 20 mA; then switch ON. IN is negative, VSYS must not become negative or supply a load. Record leakage; engineering screening bound <2 mA steady, no CC. Restore positive polarity with sources OFF. For USB testing, install the verified Pico with modules absent and use a separately current-limited 5 V USB fixture, initially 100 mA limit; stop if CC and set a larger source budget only after Pico load/inrush is accounted. Confirm USB powers VSYS with S1 OFF, battery-positive emulator at 4.25/3.70 V, and reverse emulator at −4.25 V only after the isolated reverse test passed. Record signed battery-terminal current and look for unexpected USB-dependent battery backfeed; investigate a repeatable charging current rather than treating it as a charger. USB bypasses F1/U2, so battery fault tests cannot approve host current or output-short behavior. Never test a USB output short from a computer port.
7. **Pico/modules, then cell gate:** sources OFF, positive emulator, USB unplugged. Install verified firmware and Pico alone; start at 4.20 V/0.20 A limit. Add mapped touch/buzzer cables one at a time with power OFF, accounting for measured current before any limit increase, and cap normal battery input at 0.70 A. Check HIGH buzzer idle/reset silence, notes/releases/modifiers, all touch LEDs, both ST0238 modules sounding and intended expansion. Require ≤250 mA external 3V3 and ≤350 mA total including Pico; measure at the correct rail, not infer from battery current. Engineering steady 3V3 screening range is **3.14–3.46 V**, plus successful actual module operation. Repeat load and thermal checks at 3.70 V and just above this unit's measured UVLO. Stop playing before USB source changes; reset/reboot is allowed, settle before resuming. Capture IN/VSYS/3V3 startup and transients against actual part limits; typical TI timing is not a maximum guarantee.

**First real-cell operation is held until** the new 18650 cold fit, harness polarity, all normal-load/rail checks and reverse/fault tests above pass, and the exact **P1835J lot/terminal and external-charger compatibility** gaps in [18650-handoff.md](18650-handoff.md) are resolved. Then, with every source OFF, verify actual polarity/voltage at the disconnected harness before carrier connection. Record cell/charger lot and limits; first operation is attended. Remove the cell for external charging. UVLO stopping is the instruction to switch OFF/recharge, not to continue through repeated rebounds; cell is removed for storage.

## Analysis completed without physical parts

- U2 conduction model at 0.70 A using the published 45 mΩ ceiling (specified at 3 A): **31.5 mV, 22.1 mW**. Adding 4.25 V ×0.610 mA quiescent gives about 2.6 mW input overhead under table conditions. These do not establish PCB temperature; no four-layer datasheet thermal resistance is transferred to this two-layer carrier.
- F1 lists **330 mV typical at 1.25 A**, not a maximum. A constant-resistance illustration gives 0.264 Ω and **185 mV /129 mW at 0.70 A**; fuse heating is nonlinear, so measure rather than use this as a guaranteed drop. NKK silver-contact initial rating gives 10 mΩ maximum, or 7 mV /4.9 mW at 0.70 A; it does not bound aged contacts/inrush.
- NKK recommends a **0.1 A at 2 V AC/DC minimum** for these silver contacts. Actual instrument idle/startup current is unknown; low-load switching and contact endurance remain open qualifications. The bare-board tests below that minimum do not establish lifetime. Do not substitute the gold logic-contact version: its 0.1 A applicable upper range cannot carry this 0.70 A branch.
- MPD's revision G drawing specifies **two factory 150±5 mm AWG24 leads**. Using the [Alpha Wire conductor chart](https://cdn.belden.com/-/media/Project/AlphaWire/AlphaWire/Content/Part-Number-Color-Codes/AWG-Conversion.pdf?rev=56877f4eec5844afa71ceadb1af50b66) value of 83.1 mΩ/m for **7/32 tinned copper**, the 0.30 m nominal loop gives **24.9 mΩ, 17.5 mV /12.2 mW at 0.70 A**; two 155 mm leads give **25.8 mΩ, 18.0 mV /12.6 mW** in that same model. MPD does not specify this conductor construction/resistance, so neither result is an MPD maximum or measured harness resistance. Exclude crimps/holder/temperature from this illustration and measure the complete path; its losses precede IN and can make raw-cell cutoff higher than 3.221 V.
- The 30 mV OUT-to-socket allowance at 0.70 A implies ≤**42.9 mΩ** copper/connection resistance after U2. At the conservative 2.992 V VSYS operating point, 350 mA at 3V3 requires approximately **0.515 A** with assumed 75% conversion efficiency; reaching 0.70 A corresponds to about 55% efficiency before other loads. Measured rail currents/efficiency still govern acceptance.
- C2/C4 total 9.4 µF nominal, after −10% initial and −15% temperature gives 7.19 µF before bias/aging. Keeping >1 µF requires >**13.9% combined bias/aging retention**. This computes needed margin; it does not turn a typical manufacturer DC-bias curve into a guaranteed minimum. Confirm effective capacitance from the actual parts/bias conditions. All VSYS additions including Pico remain ≤100 µF; no hold-up bank.
- No live-cell fault clearing, holder ampacity, runtime or formal single-fault certification is derived. F1 protects input-side shorts as a slow backup; a short ahead of it still depends on the protected cell. A VSYS short with USB present also draws through Pico's USB path; U2 does not protect that source.

Ratings/threshold sources are linked in [branch-protection.md](branch-protection.md): TI SLVSFC9C, SCHURTER UMT-H, NKK MN and TDK characterization.
