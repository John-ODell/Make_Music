## Bulk capacitance and review heuristics

The selected original Pico H includes the original Pico regulator circuit. The [official Pico datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/pico/pico-datasheet.pdf), Appendix B Figure 19 (Rev3 schematic), shows **RT6150B-33GQW with 47 µF at VSYS input and 47 µF at 3V3 output**, each 6.3 V X5R. The [Richtek datasheet](https://www.richtek.com/assets/product_file/RT6150A=RT6150B/DS6150AB-06.pdf), page 10, recommends a minimum 10 µF output ceramic, near VOUT/GND, and describes larger capacitance as reducing ripple and load-step voltage drop. Thus the carrier-only “no ≥10 µF 3V3 bulk” heuristic does not demonstrate a missing regulator capacitor. Nominal values do not establish effective capacitance under bias or transient compliance.

**PM CAD recommendation before final export:** add one **10 µF ceramic from 3V3_OUT to GND beside each buzzer header J13/J14**, retaining existing 100 nF bypass. Exact candidate: [TDK C2012X7R1A106K125AC](https://product.tdk.com/en/search/capacitor/ceramic/mlcc/info?part_no=C2012X7R1A106K125AC), 10 µF ±10%, 10 V, X7R, 0805, 1.25 mm nominal thickness; check the manufacturer's land guidance. This modest addition is warranted as a precaution for two pulsed loads fed over the carrier and cables. It is not a substitute for adequate regulator current or module-end bypass, and 10 µF is a starting value, not a demonstrated requirement. Check [TDK's bias/temperature characterization](https://product.tdk.com/en/system/files/dam/doc/product/capacitor/ceramic/mlcc/charasheet/c2012x7r1a106k125ac.pdf) and qualify startup/ringing/transients. The additional 20 µF is on **3V3**, not the ≤100 µF VSYS node; it still contributes to regulator startup demand. No CAD change is made in this audit. If adopted, regenerate checks, BOM, SMT centroid and manifest; baseline counts below will increase by two SMT capacitors.

For each touch board, [Tontek's application circuit](https://www.tontek.com.tw/uploads/product/243/TTP223-BA6_V2.1_EN.pdf), page 6 of 8, requires 100 nF close to IC VDD/VSS. Carrier C1–C12 are before the cables. Inspect actual module-end decoupling and add it to the module/harness assembly scope if absent. Scope at the modules during simultaneous buzzer pulses, touch LED transitions and source changes; required load/current/ripple evidence is still missing.

TP1 is a bare VBUS test pad and adds no active load requiring its own bypass capacitor. Pico already has the VBUS-to-VSYS diode/regulator-input network. A dedicated carrier 3V3 test point would help factory probing, but existing 3V3/GND header contacts provide measurement access; absence of a dedicated pad is not a connection defect. H1 is bottom-side **THT**, so the dual-SMT-assembly warning does not apply. These dispositions address the reported review heuristics; they do not claim that this helper executed the PM's live Konnect review.
