# Cassette interfaces — independent drawing work completed

Updated 2026-10-09. The editable [generator](cassette_interfaces.py) and [parameter record](cassette-interfaces.json) produce two nominal DXF interface drawings and a [plan/section review](cassette-interface-review.svg). These are **review CAD, not released machining/CAM instructions or a completed enclosure**. They complete geometry that is independent of the unmeasured modules and exact protected cell. No module mounting holes, extra carrier holes or native KiCad changes are introduced.

The drawings retain the established **legacy** mechanical datum native (20,20). For the proposed 330 x121.5 board, its rear is legacy y=-1.5; cassette x145..257/y12..48 and all mounting centres stay fixed in that frame. If root adopts the new y18.5 outline's top-left origin, the offset becomes **(+20,+18.5)** and cassette y becomes **13..49**; all native positions stay unchanged. See [datum table](DATUMS.md). The DXFs use **x right/y up = (legacy mechanical x, -legacy mechanical y)**, units millimetres. Nominal model z is positive **down** from carrier underside; the upper fixture uses a different z datum.

## Interfaces from known evidence

The 112 x36 insulating backplate has the four existing carrier-compatible nominal 3.2 holes at (150,17),(210,17),(150,43),(210,43), z3..5. Minimum hole-centre-to-outline margin is 5; Ø8 hardware projections fit with 1 mm nominal rim beyond them. The four holes retain a 60 x26 centre pattern. A proposed 94 x26 lower platform occupies x154..248/y17..43, z14..16, supporting the tolerance-expanded holder body with 7.90 beyond each end and 2.30 per side. Platform size is an engineering proposal, not a manufacturer requirement. Its attachment to the cassette is intentionally not invented in these files.

[MPD BH-18650-W drawing G, 2024-08-21](https://www.batteryholders.com/uploads/parts/BH-18650-W/datasheets/BH-18650-W-datasheet.pdf) supplies nominal body 77.70 x20.90 x21.31 and general decimal +/-0.5. The review uses body maximum 78.20 x21.40 x21.81 and holder base plane z16. The two nominal platform hole references are **(172.83,30),(228.44,30)**: nominal left body x162.15 +10.68, then +55.61. MPD shows through diameter 3.20, recess diameter 5.08 and recommends 2-56 machine screws or the specified eyelet family. The DXF's holder holes are on **HOLDER_HOLES_PENDING**, visually distinct from the retained carrier interface.

The general +/-0.5 also applies to the 10.68 and 55.61 dimensions unless another tolerance is established. Their chained second-centre reference can differ by +/-1.0 relative to the illustrated body-end datum, before installed-body positioning. The nominal centres are not a precision matched-hole guarantee. Factory must confirm the delivered view, hole pitch/recess, screw head/length and contact/cell clearance before deciding final round holes, slots, inserts or an alternate fastening method. No tapping, screw-head fit, torque or strength is claimed.

The platform z14 upper face is nominal 4.1 below the M3x12 tip limit z9.9. Right carrier hardware overlaps the wider holder in plan; the lowered platform separates it in height. The diagram reserves the existing Ø8 hardware volumes down to z12, adding 2.1 below the nominal tip. **z12 is an engineering clearance limit, not a verified bolt envelope.** No proposed platform/support/strain-relief feature may enter a measured hardware volume plus >=2 clearance. Closed-shell rails, ribs and attachment locations remain a factory design/inspection task; this model does not hide a collision behind a plan view.

Door reference z42..44 and complete fitted/loaded envelope limit z40 leave >=2 internally. Maximum bare holder ends z37.81, leaving only 2.19 additional allowance for fitted cell/wires/fasteners/tolerance/deflection. Supports >=47 leave 3 nominal to the surface. These remain stack targets, not measured fit or a validated retainer. The holder's drawn cut-down sides must remain accessible when the instrument is lifted, disconnected and the service door is removed.

## What is finished, and what remains

Finished independent work: retained 4-hole carrier pattern; nominal backplate/platform outlines and stack; manufacturer-derived holder hole references with explicit tolerances; body/bolt/door/feet review envelopes; machine-readable parameters; reproducible DXF and SVG coordinates. Root's held 806457b IPC export confirms all four mounts and the moved H1 native (289,40), bottom/0 degrees. This is a review of that held export, not a fresh CAD readback or fitted harness test.

Still pending: exact protected-cell fit/removal and holder ampacity, actual screw tips and holder mounting fit, all dressed leads/strain relief, shell/rib joints and 47 mm right overhang load path, positive door latch/cell retention, material/flatness/machining tolerance, feet/support stability, and >=2 actual loaded clearance throughout. Do not machine the holder hole references or replicate an unqualified first instrument merely because DXF is supplied. The lower platform and backplate alone do not form a closed, retained battery assembly. Factory qualification applies to the first instrument within the selected five and acceptance then to each complete instrument; optional sixth is bare only.

No ordinary published geometry requires moving the four carrier mounts, upper fixture, left-hand modifiers or GPIO. Retain the full enlarged cassette review envelope and inspect underside parts/tails/wires: the old smaller copper rule area is explicitly partial. No further module-seat geometry is defensible before real outline/thickness/bare-edge/header metrology; the existing fixture proposal already preserves independent nine-hole mounting and all controls.

## Reproduce and inspect

Run `python3 hardware/mechanical/cassette_interfaces.py --out-dir hardware/mechanical` in this isolated worktree. The generator uses only the parameter JSON and Python standard library. DXFs have millimetre INSUNITS, ordinary LINE/CIRCLE/TEXT entities, named review layers and a visible REVIEW ONLY note. They contain no cutter compensation or tolerance information beyond this document. Use the SVG for datum/stack review; factory converts accepted measured geometry into its toleranced process CAD.

Validation on 2026-10-09: generator ran successfully; independent ezdxf reader confirmed millimetre units, closed four-edge outlines, retained/nominal hole coordinates and **zero audit errors or repairs** for both DXFs. All five updated SVGs passed XML parsing and were rendered/visually inspected. Component/hole rectangle/circle attributes in the three board plans were compared to the previous commit and remained identical; only the rear outline/size and annotations changed. Root 806457b exported socket pads and four cassette mounts agree with the reviewed datums. No new native CAD/DRC or physical acceptance test was run here.
